# 1. Product brief
## Problem and target users
The prescription experience between patient and doctor can be frustrating leaving both unsatisfied. The process can be streamlined to eliminate these issues. Patients face inaccessible prescription modification, and untailored prescriptions. Doctors face reliance on patient descriptions of symptoms.

Healthwatch is a healthcare platform that tracks symptoms for patients and doctors. Patients can log side effects or reactions associated with medication use. Doctors can use this logging to quickly pinpoint what modifications they can make for the patient. 

The primary users for Healthwatch is the elderly or people living with a chronic condition. Medical professionals are a necessary secondary user.

## MVP
For this course project, the EHR data is seeded by the team. Healthwatch will not be integrated with other healthcare applications to receive data. Neither will Healthwatch comply with the regulations necessary to legally handle medical data.

The symptom logging workflow can be completed with a synthetic doctor and synthetic patient in a shared deployed environment. It will require the development of an adequate risk model
## Key Features

### Must-Have
- Roles
- Medication list
- Symptom logs
- Dashboard that highlights trends and flags adverse reaction.
- Ticket queue based on severity

### Should-Have
- Priority-based secure doctor/patient messaging
- Instant appointment booking

### Could-Have
- Push notifications for patient for pill intake
- Biometric data integration
- A drop-menu menu of symptoms patients could log

### Won’t-have
- Open-response text boxes to data log 
- Appointment requests that get sent to doctors via email

## Assumptions & Constraints
- HIPPA compliance is unnecessary for the purposes of the demo.
- Integration with Healthcare applications is outside the scope of this project.
